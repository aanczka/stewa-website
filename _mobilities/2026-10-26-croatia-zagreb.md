### Days 1–2

Our first two days of the Erasmus+ mobility in Zagreb, Croatia, were full of inspiration, creativity, and opportunities to learn from one another!

We started our journey by exploring different ways of combining art and environmental awareness. Through a variety of creative workshops, participants discovered how everyday materials can be reused and transformed into something beautiful and meaningful.

We created beautiful sunflowers, symbols of hope, positivity, and our connection with nature. We also experimented with painting using coffee and tea as natural colours, discovering that even simple everyday ingredients can become a source of artistic inspiration!

Another highlight was an interesting presentation entitled “The Human Body in Modern Life – How Everyday Habits Affect Our Health and Environment”, delivered by our Polish partner. It encouraged us to reflect on how our daily choices influence both our well-being and the world around us.

We also visited the Ethnographic Museum, where we participated in a fascinating cyanotype workshop. Through this traditional photographic technique, we discovered how sunlight, natural materials, and creativity can come together to create beautiful blue artworks.

### Days 3–4

Our third day of the Erasmus+ mobility in Croatia was all about creativity, imagination, and sustainable thinking!

We took part in workshops focused on making bookmarks from recycled materials and creating candles. These activities gave us the opportunity to explore new artistic techniques and discover how materials that might otherwise be thrown away can be transformed into useful and beautiful objects.

Working together, exchanging ideas, and experimenting with different materials reminded us that recycling can be both practical and artistic. Every object has the potential for a second life when creativity meets imagination!

The final day of our mobility was filled with more inspiring creative activities and wonderful shared experiences!

During the workshops, participants created beautiful owls using recycled materials, including old newspapers. We also took part in other creative activities in which everyday objects were transformed into something completely new, proving once again that waste can become a valuable resource for artistic expression.

These activities encouraged us to think creatively about sustainability, reuse, and the importance of protecting our environment.

The mobility concluded with a special certificate-awarding ceremony, celebrating the knowledge, skills, and experiences gained throughout our international meeting. Participants received their certificates, marking the end of an unforgettable Erasmus+ experience.

What an amazing journey filled with creativity, learning, cultural exchange, new friendships, and unforgettable memories!

Together, we discovered that art can inspire positive change, that recycling can be beautiful, and that even small everyday actions can make a difference for our planet.

Different countries, different cultures, one common goal: saving our planet through ART, creativity, and sustainable actions!

Erasmus+ – Enriching lives, opening minds
