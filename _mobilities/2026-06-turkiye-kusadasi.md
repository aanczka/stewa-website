### Days 1–2

Participants visited a special education school and learned about the Turkish education system. They took part in training sessions on **Outdoor Learning** and **Experiential Learning**, followed by recycling workshops for sport equipment: teams explored ways to use recycled materials in educational and sports activities, promoting creativity, teamwork, environmental awareness and sustainable practices. The second day also included coordinator meetings, project evaluation and work on the project e-journal.

### Days 3–4

The programme continued with practical outdoor and sports-based activities that promoted teamwork, environmental awareness and healthy lifestyles. Participants used the recycled sports equipment created earlier in cooperative games, and enjoyed cultural visits to the ancient city of Ephesus and the House of the Virgin Mary.

On the final day the group hiked in Dilek Peninsula National Park, with trekking activities in the forest and cooperative sports activities in nature. The mobility closed with an official farewell dinner, where Europass Mobility and participation certificates were awarded.
