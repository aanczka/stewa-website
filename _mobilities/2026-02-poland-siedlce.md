Teachers from Poland, Romania, Croatia, Türkiye, Estonia and North Macedonia participated in an international mobility dedicated to environmental education.

During the four days of activities, participants:

- took part in the training programme **“Empowering Educators for a Greener Tomorrow”**;
- exchanged best practices in ecological education;
- worked in international teams;
- developed sustainable educational activities for students;
- used digital tools and the eTwinning platform;
- created a joint environmental education programme that will be implemented in the partner schools.

Through this project we promote environmental responsibility and develop essential skills for a greener future.
