During the third and fourth days of the mobility, participants took part in practical outdoor and sports-based activities that promoted teamwork, environmental awareness, and healthy lifestyles. They used the sports equipment created from recycled materials in cooperative games, demonstrating how sustainability can be successfully integrated into physical education. The programme also included a cultural visit to the ancient city of Ephesus and the House of the Virgin Mary, providing participants with the opportunity to explore Türkiye’s rich historical and cultural heritage.

On the fourth day, participants travelled to the Dilek Peninsula National Park, where they joined trekking activities in the forest and cooperative sports activities in a natural environment. These activities highlighted the importance of outdoor learning, experiential education, and environmental protection. The mobility concluded with an official farewell dinner, during which Europass Mobility and participation certificates were awarded, celebrating the successful completion of the programme and strengthening international cooperation among the project partners.
