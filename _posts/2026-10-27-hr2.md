Our second day of the Erasmus+ mobility in Zagreb, Croatia, was another inspiring journey of learning, creativity, and cultural discovery!
We started the day with an interesting presentation: “The Human Body in Modern Life – How Everyday Habits Affect Our Health and Environment”, delivered by our Polish partner Alcedo Adult Education Centre. 
The presentation encouraged us to reflect on how our daily choices influence not only our personal well-being but also the health of our planet.
The highlight of the day was our visit to the Ethnographic Museum, where we participated in a fascinating cyanotype workshop!
Through this beautiful photographic printing technique, we discovered how sunlight, natural materials, and creativity can come together to produce unique blue artworks.
