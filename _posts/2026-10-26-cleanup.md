As part of the Erasmus+ project “SAVE the EARTH with ARTS”, pupils and teachers at the “Maria Montessori” School Center for Inclusive Education in Constanța took part in a clean-up activity in the schoolyard and on the sports field.
Through active participation, pupils discovered that everyone can contribute to caring for the environment. Small actions, when shared, can make a big difference!
