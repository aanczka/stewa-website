Today we marked Earth Day with a series of creative and educational activities!

Our students actively participated in:

- planting plants and caring for nature;
- creating collages from recycled materials;
- sorting waste through play and learning;
- music and sensory activities with natural elements.

Through play, exploration and teamwork, the children learned how important it is to protect our planet and care for the environment.

**Small steps make big changes!**
