In a joint action, volunteers from Volunteers Centre Skopje and our students cleaned up the school yard, contributing to a more beautiful and healthier environment. Through this activity we promote awareness of ecology, the importance of greenery, and caring for the space we use every day.

We appeal to all neighbours to help maintain the yard and keep it clean and tidy.

**Together, we can make a difference!**
