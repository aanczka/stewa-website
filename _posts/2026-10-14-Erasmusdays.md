During Erasmus Days 2026, the students of OURC “Dr. Zlatan Sremec” – Skopje celebrated inclusion, teamwork and environmental responsibility through a special activity organised within the Erasmus+ project “Save the Earth with Arts”.
Together with students from “Partenija Zografski” School and volunteers from the Red Cross, they created and played adapted sports games using equipment made from recycled materials.
