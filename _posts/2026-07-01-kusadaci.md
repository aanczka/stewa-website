During the first two days of the mobility in Türkiye, participants visited a special education school and learned about the Turkish education system. They also took part in training sessions on Outdoor Learning and Experiential Learning, focusing on practical and active learning methods.
The second day continued with recycling workshops for sport equipment, where participants created and explored ways to use recycled materials in educational and sports activities. The activities promoted creativity, teamwork, environmental awareness and sustainable practices. The day also included a coordinator meeting about project organization and e-journal studies, followed by evaluation sessions.
