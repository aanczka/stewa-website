Students from the „Maria Montessori” Inclusive Education School Center, Constanța, Romania, participated in an activity focused on assessing and measuring their environmental behaviour.

The activity was carried out through online questionnaires created using Google Forms, which allowed students to reflect on their daily habits, attitudes and actions related to environmental protection. This activity took place within the framework of the Erasmus+ project no. 2025-1-RO01-KA220-SCH-000354913, entitled **“SAVE the Earth With Arts”**.

By completing the questionnaires, students actively engaged in evaluating their level of environmental awareness and responsibility, while also developing digital skills and critical thinking.

The results of this pre-test will be compared with a post-test at the end of the project to measure how the STEWA activities changed the students' environmental behaviour.
