Dear students and parents,

On the occasion of celebrating Earth Day, our school is organising a campaign to clean the school environment and plant flowers. We invite you to join this laudable initiative to make our school a nicer and healthier place to be.

The action will take place on **Tuesday, 21 April 2026**, according to the following schedule:

**Gathering times**

- 12:20 – 13:05 – students of the morning shift: 1.C, 2.D, 2.E and educational groups
- 13:10 – 13:55 – students of the morning shift: 1.A, 1.F, 1.G, 1.K, 1.L
- 14:00 – 14:45 – students of the afternoon shift: 1.D, 1.E, 1.H, 2.C

**Request for students and parents.** In order to further decorate our flower beds, we ask students to bring flower seedlings to school on the day of the action. Choose plants that are resilient and that will adorn our yard during the upcoming warm days.

**Note.** Dress appropriately for outdoor work. The school will provide gloves and waste bags, and your good will and the flower you bring will make a difference!

Let's take care of our planet together, starting from our own backyard!
