First day of our mobility in Croatia!
Creativity, Sustainability & European Friendship!
What a wonderful start to our Erasmus+ mobility in Croatia!
Today, we explored the beauty of nature through art, creativity, and sustainable practices.
We created beautiful sunflowers, symbols of hope, positivity, and our connection with nature.
We also discovered a different way of painting, using coffee and tea as natural colours, turning simple everyday ingredients into unique works of art!
These creative activities reminded us that art doesn't always need expensive materials – just imagination, inspiration, and a little love for our planet!
A day filled with creativity, smiles, shared experiences, and new friendships!
Different countries, different cultures, one common goal: saving our planet through ART!
Erasmus+ – Enriching lives, opening minds!
