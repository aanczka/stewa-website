On the occasion of Erasmus Days, as part of the Erasmus+ project “Save the Earth with Arts”, the students of the “Maria Montessori” School Centre for Inclusive Education, Constanța, took part in a creative recycling workshop entitled “A New Life for Materials: We Create and Protect Nature.”
The activity aimed to develop students’ green skills by creating useful and decorative objects from reusable materials. The participants transformed paper, cardboard, plastic containers and other clean and safe recyclable materials into pencil holders, flower pots, decorations and toys.
Through practical activities adapted to each student’s abilities and individual needs, the children learned about the importance of waste reduction, selective collection and material reuse. At the same time, the workshop encouraged creativity, practical skills, teamwork and environmental responsibility, showing students that everyday materials can be given a new life through imagination and care for nature.!
