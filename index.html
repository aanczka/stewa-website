Erasmus+ · KA220-SCH · 6 partners in 6 countries

Environmental awareness and climate action, through the arts

The project aims to promote environmental awareness and climate action among students with special educational needs (SEN), their families, and educators. Through a range of training activities, workshops, and collaborative events, participants deepen their understanding of climate change and sustainable living.

We support teachers in developing innovative, creative and digital approaches to environmental education, while fostering greater inclusion and participation of SEN students.
